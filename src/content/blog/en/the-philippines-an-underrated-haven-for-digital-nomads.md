# Digital Nomad Visa Philippines

Are you a digital nomad looking to explore the Philippines? The country offers a range of exciting destinations, beautiful beaches, and a vibrant culture. But have you considered the requirements for a digital nomad visa in the Philippines?

**What is a digital nomad visa?** A digital nomad visa is a type of visa that allows remote workers to live and work in a foreign country for an extended period. The Philippines is one of the countries that offer this type of visa.

**How to apply for a digital nomad visa in the Philippines**

To apply for a digital nomad visa in the Philippines, you will need to meet the requirements set by the Philippine government. These requirements include having a valid passport, a return ticket, and proof of sufficient funds. You will also need to have a job or a business that allows you to work remotely.
